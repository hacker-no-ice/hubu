## Experiments

| Experiment | Verdict | Observed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The runaway agent (`ep01-runaway`) | Holds | 10 ledger postings consumed $0.10. 100 authorization attempts were denied; 0 remained unresolved. |
| The last cent (`ep02-last-cent`) | Holds with caveat | $0.03 recorded against $0.01 authorized; $0.02 overrun visible. 11 new requests were denied. The public claim records owner reconciliation. |
| The unresolved crash (`ep03-crash-recovery`) | Holds | $0.01 remained frozen after restart. Final accounting contains 1 posting totaling $0.01, with owner and retry checks recorded separately. |
| Many clients, one budget (`ep04-many-clients`) | Holds with caveat | 33 allowed, 67 denied, 0 unresolved. $0.99 reserved during the burst; $0.99 consumed after settlement. |

## Boundaries

- **The runaway agent:** The budget and policy stay fixed throughout. These results do not establish authority boundaries for a caller with the broad administrative bearer.
- **The last cent:** Full overrun accounting requires explicit owner reconciliation after lease expiry. A negative remaining budget correctly records the confirmed charge above the estimate.
- **The unresolved crash:** The fault is a Hubu process kill after a confirmed claim and synthetic billed marker, before settlement. Provider execution, disk loss, and Gongbu recovery are outside this test.
- **Many clients, one budget:** Ten client processes create the network burst. The released server handles connections serially; this does not measure simultaneous SQLite writers, throughput, or fairness.

## Known limits

- Fixture receipts control cost and timing. They do not establish real invoices, truthful
  executors, provider-side exactly-once execution or task quality. Gongbu, Temporal, MCP
  transport, provider adapters and actual provider billing are outside these kits.
